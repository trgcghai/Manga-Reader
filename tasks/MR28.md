# MR28: Reading History

## Title
Track reading history with timestamps.

## Description
Persist a reading history so the app can show recently opened chapters and surface a Continue reading section.

## Requirement
- Record when a chapter is opened
- Store timestamps for history entries
- Show a Continue reading section in the UI
- Keep history local to the app

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Opening a chapter adds it to reading history
- [ ] History entries store timestamps
- [ ] The UI shows a Continue reading section
- [ ] History persists across sessions

5
